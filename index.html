Common Health Conditions

Quick guide for everyday health concerns and home remedies

Common Cold

Viral infection of the upper respiratory tract causing runny nose, sore throat, and sneezing.

Home Remedies
  • Warm salt water gargle
  • Steam inhalation
  • Ginger tea with honey
  • Plenty of rest and fluids

Fever

Elevated body temperature — a sign the immune system is fighting infection. Needs monitoring.

Home Care
  • Stay hydrated with water & electrolytes
  • Cool compresses on the forehead
  • Light, breathable clothing
  • Rest in a well-ventilated room

Headache

Pain in the head or neck region — tension, migraine, or cluster types are most common.

Relief Tips
  • Rest in a dark, quiet room
  • Apply cold or warm compress
  • Stay well hydrated
  • Gentle neck and shoulder stretches

Our Mission

MedRAG combines Retrieval-Augmented Generation with a curated medical knowledge base to give you instant, reliable health insights — no internet required.

Please read these terms carefully before using MedRAG

1. Medical Disclaimer

MedRAG provides health information for educational purposes only. The content is not intended to be a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always seek the advice of your physician or other qualified health provider with any questions you may have regarding a medical condition.

2. User Responsibilities

Users are responsible for their health decisions and should use the information provided as a guide rather than definitive medical advice. We encourage consulting healthcare professionals for personalised medical guidance.

3. Privacy Policy

MedRAG runs entirely locally on your machine. Your symptoms and queries are sent only to the local FastAPI server (127.0.0.1:8000) and are never transmitted to external servers. No personal data is stored or shared.

4. Data Sources

All health information is retrieved from the medical PDF files stored in the backend/data/ folder. The quality and accuracy of responses depends on the quality of documents provided. Only use authoritative, peer-reviewed medical sources.
